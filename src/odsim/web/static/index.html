The model is an agent-based simulation: every person moves, may enroll in or leave treatment, and may overdose each day. Overdoses are less often fatal near naloxone or with someone else nearby, and unhoused people face higher risk and lower access to treatment. Parameters are illustrative, not calibrated to real data. Built with Python, NumPy and FastAPI; source on GitHub.
